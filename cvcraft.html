CV Crafted

is a modern resume builder designed to help job seekers create professional, ATS-friendly resumes in minutes. With customizable templates and an easy-to-use interface, CV Crafted makes it simple to showcase your skills, experience, and achievements with confidence.

Client James Park
Sector Education
Duration 2 weeks

Project Overview

I delivered a full resume-building experience: intuitive user interface design, customizable ATS-friendly templates, seamless PDF and digital exports, AI-assisted content suggestions, guided step-by-step workflows, and ongoing updates to ensure compatibility with the latest hiring standards. This is a complete career-ready solution, designed to target ATS filters and recruiters alike.

Our Approach

I combine smart design, ATS-friendly templates, and guided content tools to help users create resumes that stand out. Every feature is built to make the process simple, and career-ready.

How I Did It

Design & Templates

Built intuitive, professional, ATS-friendly resume layouts. Every template is crafted to balance visual appeal with compatibility, clearly structuring your resume stands out to recruiters at first glance.

Smart Guidance

Added AI-assisted content suggestions for skills, experience, and achievements. ResumeHub analyzes your input and provides actionable advice to strengthen every step, making it easy to write descriptions even if it's your first resume.

Export & Share

Enabled seamless PDF, docx, and cloud-ready downloads. Resumes can be shared directly, saved to the cloud, giving users flexibility and a polished, no-hassle result.
